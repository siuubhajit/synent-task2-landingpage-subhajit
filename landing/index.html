Features

Entry

Type distance, time and effort on one line. Pacelog works out the pace for you.

Track records

Mileage, sessions and rest days for each week sit on one screen.

Your log lives on your device, so you can add a run on a trail with no signal. When you get internet you can sync progress.

CSV export

Download your full history as a CSV file whenever you want to open it in a spreadsheet.
